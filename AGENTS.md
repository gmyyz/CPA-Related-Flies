# CPA Related Workspace Instructions

## Accounting Q&A Source Selection

These rules apply to all Codex tasks working in this repository.

1. For textbook screenshots and conceptual accounting explanations, reason first from the supplied image, the current conversation, applicable accounting knowledge, and existing notes in this repository. Do not browse the web by default.

2. Browse official sources for information that may have changed, including current policies, current accounting or examination requirements, CPA examination syllabuses, and current model or product information.

3. Browse when an exact examination answer is disputed, when available interpretations conflict, when a prior answer conflicts with the current conclusion, or when confidence in a material technical detail is insufficient. Briefly tell the user why verification is needed before browsing.

4. If the user explicitly asks to search, browse, verify, or look something up, follow that request.

5. When browsing, prefer primary and official sources. If an official source does not provide the exact examination answer, a reputable teaching source may be used, but identify it as an examination or teaching interpretation rather than an official accounting standard.

6. Do not browse merely to restate content that is already clear in a supplied textbook screenshot.

Quick rule:

~~~text
Textbook screenshot and concept explanation:
reason from the image, context, and accounting knowledge first.

Current policy, examination syllabus, model, or product information:
browse official sources.

Disputed examination answer, conflicting interpretations, or inconsistent prior answers:
browse to verify and explain why verification is needed.
~~~
